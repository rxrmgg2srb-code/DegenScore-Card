## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take security seriously. If you discover a security vulnerability in DegenScore, please follow these steps:

1.  **Do not create a public GitHub issue.** This allows us to fix the issue before it can be exploited.
2.  Email our security team at `security@degenscore.com`.
3.  Include a detailed description of the vulnerability and steps to reproduce it.

### Our Promise

*   We will respond to your report within 24 hours.
*   We will keep you updated on our progress in fixing the issue.
*   We will credit you (if desired) in our changelog once the fix is released.

### Bounty Program

We offer bug bounties for critical vulnerabilities on a case-by-case basis. Please include your SOL address in your report.
